What the app is

Tile Tally photographs a mahjong hand, reads the tiles, and scores it against a ruleset you choose. It keeps score across a run of hands.

It is a scoring aid, not a referee. Mahjong scoring varies enormously between regions, tables and households, and the app ships one interpretation of four traditions plus whatever you configure yourself. The tile recognition is a machine-learning model and it is sometimes wrong, which is why every hand is shown for you to check before it scores. Check the tiles and check the score. If the app and your table disagree, your table is right.

Nothing in the app is intended to support gambling, and we take no part in any stakes you play for.
